What we won't compromise on

Four rules the whole platform is built around

Hover or tap a card to see why each one is non-negotiable.

01

Consent first

Nothing lists without a signature.

Why it matters

Every will starts as a blind signal. It only enters the directory after the person themself signs an authorization link — not the partner, not us. If it isn't signed, it doesn't exist on the platform.

02

One heir, always

No bidding wars, ever.

Why it matters

The moment a will is claimed, it's gone from every other directory, permanently. We'd rather have fewer claims than let one lead get sold five times over.

03

Flat, disclosed fees

Priced before you claim, not after.

Why it matters

Success fees are set by budget tier and shown before you unlock — never negotiated after a deal closes. What you see on the will is what you'll owe.

04

Masked until claimed

You browse intent, not identity.

Why it matters

Names and contact details stay blurred until a will is claimed. You're evaluating territory, budget, and timeline — not scraping a directory of real people.
